TechFix Return and Refund Policy

Customers may return eligible products within 30 days of purchase.

Return Conditions:
- Product must be unused.
- Original packaging must be available.
- Purchase invoice is required.
- Accessories should be included.

Refund Policy:
- Refunds are processed within 7 business days after successful inspection.
- Refunds are credited to the original payment method.

Exchange Policy:
- Customers may request a replacement for defective products within 30 days.
- Replacement depends on product availability.

Products cannot be returned if:
- Physically damaged by the customer.
- Warranty seals are broken.
- Accessories are missing.
