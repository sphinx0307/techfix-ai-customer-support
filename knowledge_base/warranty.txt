TechFix Electronics Warranty Policy

All TechFix electronic products come with a standard manufacturer warranty.

Warranty Period:
- Laptops: 2 Years
- Smartphones: 1 Year
- Smartwatches: 1 Year
- Wireless Earbuds: 6 Months

The warranty covers:
- Manufacturing defects
- Hardware component failures
- Battery defects caused by manufacturing issues
- Display defects not caused by physical damage

The warranty does not cover:
- Physical damage
- Water damage
- Accidental drops
- Unauthorized repairs
- Damage caused by misuse

To claim warranty:
1. Carry the purchase invoice.
2. Bring the product to an authorized TechFix Service Center.
3. Warranty claims are verified by service engineers before approval.

If the warranty period has expired, paid repair services are available.
